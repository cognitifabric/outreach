How enquiry data is handled

Updated October 1, 2026.

What you submit

The enquiry form asks for your name, business name, email and optional phone number, plus your business type, booking setup, preferred reply method and the workflow you want help with. Campaign tags in the page URL may be included so we understand where an enquiry came from. Please do not include client records, passwords or payment information.

Why we use it

We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry. Submitting the form does not purchase a service or subscribe you to automated marketing. The interactive website examples use fictional data and do not place bookings, send SMS or process payments.

Who receives it

Enquiries are saved in the website database and are available to authorized members of our business team. Email notifications go to contact@fabricioguardia.com and any additional business partners configured by the account owner. Team members use the information to coordinate and respond to your enquiry.

Service providers

Website hosting, database and email providers process the information needed to deliver these functions and may maintain operational logs. The enquiry database does not store your IP address. Temporary per-process IP counters are used to limit repeated requests. Admin logins use essential, secure session cookies that expire after eight hours.

Questions or deletion requests

Email contact@fabricioguardia.com with your enquiry reference. We may need to verify that the enquiry belongs to you before changing or deleting it. We keep enquiry information for responding and managing our business relationship, and review it for deletion when it is no longer needed.

Instagram contact

If you choose to message @thesalonagent, Instagram also handles that conversation under its own policies.
