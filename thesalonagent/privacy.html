The Salon Agent

How enquiry data is handled

Updated October 1, 2026.

What you submit

The enquiry form asks for your name, salon name, email and optional phone number, plus information about your salon and the workflow you want help with. Please do not include customer records, passwords, payment information or other sensitive data.

Why we use it

We use your details to review and respond to your enquiry about our services. Submitting this form does not purchase a service or subscribe you to automated marketing.

Where it goes

When the form is enabled, enquiries are saved in the site's configured database. A notification may be sent to the business's configured email inbox. Website hosting, database and email providers process the information needed to deliver those functions. Hosting and email services may maintain operational logs. The form does not add your IP address to the enquiry database.

Questions or deletion requests

Message @thesalonagent on Instagram with your enquiry reference. We may need to verify that the enquiry belongs to you before changing or deleting it.

Instagram contact

If you choose to message us on Instagram, that conversation is also handled by Instagram under its own policies.
