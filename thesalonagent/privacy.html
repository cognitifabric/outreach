Privacy policy

Updated October 14, 2026.

This policy covers enquiries and demo/support messaging for The Salon Agent through this website and our business number, +1 (323) 694-2465. A salon's own customer messaging is a separate service and must use its own consent and privacy practices.

What we collect

The enquiry form asks for your name and email, an optional business name and phone number, your business type, booking setup, preferred reply method and the workflow you want help with. Campaign tags in the page URL may be included so we understand where an enquiry came from. Please do not include client records, passwords or payment information.

If you choose SMS updates, we also save your consent choice, mobile number, a server-recorded timestamp, the form source, the consent statement and its version. A phone number or a phone-call preference by itself does not grant permission for ongoing text messages.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to questions about our software and services, arrange demos, manage requested follow-up and provide customer support. With consent, texts may include demo confirmations, reminders, rescheduling or cancellation updates and answers to your service questions. Human team members and configured automated or AI assistants may help with those conversations. This form does not enroll you in promotional text campaigns.

Submitting an enquiry does not purchase a service. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. The interactive website examples use fictional data and do not place bookings, send SMS or process payments.

SMS privacy and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or rent mobile numbers, text-message opt-in data or consent. SMS consent is not transferred to another business, shared for that business's messaging or used to enroll you in another campaign.

We share only information needed with service providers that process communications on our behalf, such as messaging, hosting, database, email and configured AI providers, to deliver the requested service and honor your choices. These providers do not receive permission to use your SMS consent for their own marketing. Consent records are restricted to authorized business staff and processing necessary to operate the program.

Who can access an enquiry

Enquiries are saved in the website database and are available to authorized members of our business team. Email notifications go to contact@fabricioguardia.com and any additional business partners configured by the account owner. Those recipients coordinate and respond to your enquiry; they do not receive permission to enroll you in their own text campaigns.

Your messaging choices

Message frequency varies with your requests and appointments. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts or HELP for help. You can also contact contact@fabricioguardia.com. Opting out of texts does not prevent email contact. Read the SMS terms for program details.

Storage, security and cookies

Hosting, database and communication providers process information needed for their functions and may maintain operational logs. The enquiry database does not store your IP address. Temporary per-process IP counters limit repeated requests. Admin logins use essential, secure session cookies that expire after eight hours. We restrict team access and use HTTPS, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and requests

We keep enquiry information while responding and managing our business relationship, and review it for deletion when it is no longer needed. Limited consent or opt-out records may be retained to honor your preferences and demonstrate permission. Email contact@fabricioguardia.com with your enquiry reference to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify that the enquiry belongs to you.

Other websites and updates

If you contact @thesalonagent, Instagram also handles that conversation under its own policies. The separate website at thesalonagent.com has its own site privacy notice. We update the date above when this policy changes.
