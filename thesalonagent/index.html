BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can it help after we close?

The automated workflow is designed to answer routine calls and texts 24/7, using your configured salon information and booking rules. Human callbacks follow your team’s availability.

Do we need to replace our booking software?

We check your existing booking, calendar and payment tools first. Compatibility is confirmed in your proposal. Replacing a full booking platform is a separate scope, rather than an assumption in the monthly service.

Can it book with a specific stylist?

Our salon workflow supports multiple team members, staff-specific service prices, working hours and breaks. We configure and test the availability rules with your calendars before launch.

How do deposits work?

The workflow can send a deposit link and display payment status. Your payment provider processes the funds. The provider connection, status updates and customer confirmation messages must pass an end-to-end test before we launch that payment workflow.

What if a customer needs a person?

The current workflow flags the request and captures a callback for your team. Live transfer is not currently enabled. We agree the follow-up process and human support expectations with you.

What is included in $699/month?

The single-location managed service starts at $699/month. Setup charges, included voice and text usage, connected tools, support and extra-usage rates are set out in a written proposal. Simultaneous call capacity is scoped separately; unlimited calls are not included.
