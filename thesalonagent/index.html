THE QUESTIONS BEHIND THE DECISION

Let’s be clear
about the details.

Can it book appointments directly?

Our custom implementation includes appointment booking against connected staff calendars. For your business, we test your booking platform and permissions first. If direct access is unavailable, the starting workflow can send a booking link or collect a callback request.

Will clients know they are speaking to AI?

Yes. The assistant identifies itself, follows your approved greeting and answers, and offers a route to a person or callback.

Can we keep our phone number?

We check your phone provider and routing options. A supported forwarding setup may let clients continue using your existing number.

What happens when something needs a person?

We define a handoff before launch. Your assistant can collect the request and contact details for the team instead of guessing or overriding a policy.

What does setup cost, and how long does it take?

We quote the workflow and expected usage after reviewing your setup. You see setup costs, monthly fees, included usage, overage terms and a tested rollout plan before deciding.

Is this only for salons?

Our starting experience is in lash and beauty workflows. We can assess other appointment businesses with similar scheduling and enquiry needs, and agree which capabilities fit their operations.
