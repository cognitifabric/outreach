BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can it help after we close?

The automated front desk can answer routine calls and texts after hours using your business information and booking rules. Human callbacks follow your team’s availability.

Do we need to replace our booking software?

We review your existing booking, calendar and payment tools first. Compatible connections are confirmed in your proposal. Replacing an entire booking platform is a separate scope.

Can it book with a specific employee?

The workflow checks employee availability and configured service prices. We test your working hours, breaks and calendar connections before launch.

How do deposits work?

The system sends a payment link and records deposit status. Your payment provider processes the funds. We verify payment updates and the appointment confirmation rules as part of setup.

What if a customer needs a person?

The workflow flags the request and captures a callback for your team. Live call transfer is not currently enabled. We agree how your team will handle those requests.

What is included in $699/month?

The single-location managed service starts at $699/month. Setup charges, included voice and text usage, connected tools, support and extra-usage rates are set out in a written proposal. Simultaneous call capacity is scoped separately; unlimited calls are not included.
