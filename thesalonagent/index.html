A CLEAR FIT BEFORE YOU COMMIT.

Your questions,
answered.

We’ll map the right scope for your business before launch.

Can it replace my receptionist?

It can take over the routine phone, text and booking workflow. The right scope depends on your business. In-person reception, complex complaints and decisions requiring a person still need your team.

Do I have to replace my booking software?

We first check which booking, calendar and payment tools can connect. Keeping your current system may be the best fit. Replacing a full platform is a separate project; compatibility is confirmed before you sign up.

Does it handle payments?

Configured workflows can send payment links and update deposit status through supported integrations. Funds are processed by your payment provider. Refunds, disputes and other financial decisions follow your approval rules.

How many calls can it take at once?

Capacity is sized to your business. Ten, twenty, thirty or one hundred simultaneous calls are different configurations with different costs. Monthly call minutes and simultaneous capacity are quoted separately; 100-call capacity is not included automatically in the $799 starting plan.

What if the AI cannot answer?

It routes the exception to a person or captures a callback request under your rules. Your automated front desk is available 24/7; human support hours and response expectations are agreed in your proposal.

What does the monthly price include?

Your proposal sets out the included voice and text usage, connected tools, setup work, ongoing management and any additional fees. High-volume capacity, extra locations and custom development are scoped separately.
